USER: What engine did the MG F use and why did it have reliability issues?  
ASSISTANT: The MG F used the K Series engine, which gained a reputation for head gasket failure. This was mainly due to the thermostat being placed on the intake side of the water pump at the base of the block, causing the head to overheat before the thermostat opened. Additional factors included insufficient cylinder width, build tolerances, block stiffness, and the use of plastic dowels that melted when overheating. A redesigned MLS head gasket and steel dowels helped reduce the problem.

USER: When did production of the MG F end?  
ASSISTANT: Production of the MG F ended in March 2011. After Nanjing took over the company and restarted production, sales declined significantly, leading to the eventual cessation of manufacturing.

USER: What changes were made to fix the head gasket issue in the K Series engine?  
ASSISTANT: The head gasket problem was addressed by using a redesigned multiple layer metal gasket (MLS) and steel locating dowels instead of the original plastic ones. This change helped prevent the head gasket from "walking" due to overheating. Some owners also modified the system by relocating the thermostat to the outlet pipe for better reliability.
