ASSISTANT: The MG N-type had a new chassis that was wider at the rear than the front, a departure from the ladder-type chassis used in earlier models. The body was mounted on outriggers off the main frame and was taller with rear-hinged doors and cut-away tops. The fuel tank was now hidden in the tail, unlike the visible slab tank on earlier models.

USER: When was the MG N-type produced, and what was its engine like?  
ASSISTANT: The MG N-type was produced from October 1934 to 1936. It had a 1271 cc 6-cylinder overhead camshaft engine derived from the K-Type and originally used in the 1930 Wolseley Hornet. The engine was modified with a new cylinder block and head and featured twin SU carburettors, producing 56 bhp at 5500 rpm.
